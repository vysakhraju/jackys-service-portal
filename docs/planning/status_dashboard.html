Phase 10, in detail

Defective/DOA appliance recovery — harvest, verify, price-and-post, with a real three-actor segregation of duties

Second of the three post-MVP modules you asked for (AMC → Dismantling → Reports/Dashboards). A DismantlingRecord tracks a write-off appliance already in Damage Location through strip-down, logged components (a jsonb snapshot, cross-checked against the existing ComponentYieldMatrix BOM/yield master data), supervisor verification, and finally the Service Manager's manual pricing + posting — which atomically increases live spare-part stock and posts a GL entry (AC-30). The three steps must be three different people: the API rejects (400, not just a role check) if the same account tries to verify its own harvest, or price-and-post something it harvested or verified.
